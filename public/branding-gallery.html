A quieter home for the schema canvas.

This is not a new visual universe. It is a focused product expression of Nightingale: the same ink, paper, bird, rufous signal, and type roles—with almost everything removed so the canvas can lead.

Trade-off

The glyph reads well at app-icon size, but it creates a second identity mark. If family consistency matters more than product independence, use the bird favicon from direction 01 and keep this motif only inside thumbnails.

Best use

The dark route works especially well for link previews and launch imagery. For the editor itself, follow the user’s stored light/dark preference rather than making darkness part of the product identity.
